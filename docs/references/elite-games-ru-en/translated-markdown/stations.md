Orbital stations

All types of orbital stations present in the game and their features are described here.

Starbases and orbital forts

All orbital stations are divided into two classes: starbases and orbital forts. Starbases include: “Space Station”, “Space Dock”, “Ultra Station”, “Death Star”. Only “Orbital Fort” refers to orbital forts.

The orbital fort is designed to protect the planet from possible attack, and it can also accommodate one orbital device - a stargate or a packet driver. Starbases, in addition to performing defense functions, are capable of building ships and refueling and repairing ships.

At starbases, ships are more efficiently dismantled for minerals/resources and technology transfer is possible accordingly.

In the Scores rating, orbital stations of the starbase type are taken into account when calculating starbases; forts are not taken into account (and accordingly do not affect the number of points).

Features of orbital stations

There can either be a station in the orbit of a planet or not (there can be no more than one station).

Starbases of the “Space Dock” type are capable of building ships only weighing up to 200 kt inclusive. Other types of starbases are capable of building ships of any tonnage.

In combat, the orbital station is the only stationary object. Accordingly, she cannot leave the battlefield and/or catch up with the enemy. For the same reason, she cannot initiate a battle against another ship.

In combat, the orbital station is considered a separate ship. This means that if there is a message “there was a battle in orbit 5 vs 7”, then the orbital station is one of the 5 ships.

The orbital station repairs itself at a rate of 10% per year (or 15% for IS) and assistance from SFX class ships is impossible. If you upgrade a station, the new type of station will inherit the level of damage from the old hull. If a faster repair method is needed, then you can remove the design (all stations of a given type will be destroyed) and build a new orbital station, which will no longer be damaged.

If colonists leave the planet, the orbital station self-destructs.

Advantages of orbital stations

The cost of all components (except for orbital ones) built into the station is calculated as 50% of the base cost.

Any orbital station prevents the bombing of colonists, landings and negative terraforming. That is, until the orbital station is destroyed, it will be impossible to start bombing, landing troops or deterraforming the planet - the bombers will hang around uselessly in orbit, the landing force will be destroyed when attempting to land and disappear into nowhere.

Orbital stations are the only elements that can use packet drivers, and are also the only receivers for ship intergate jumps.

If a packet driver is built into the station, it protects against enemy packets (partially or completely).

If the type of base hull is changed during an upgrade or downgrade, then half of the resources and minerals from the previous design are inherited. Otherwise, all resources and minerals are preserved.

Disadvantages of orbital stations

The station cannot be destroyed by standard means. It can either be destroyed by the enemy, or deleted as a design (accordingly, all stations of the same design will be deleted), or as a result of colonists leaving the planet.

For starbases (without a fort), the effectiveness of jamming (missile deflection devices) has been reduced. For starbases, jamming approaches 75%, for ships and forts - to 95%.

General classification of orbital stations

There are two lines:

1. Orbital Fort, Space Dock - Fast-to-produce orbital stations that provide basic defense. They can integrate one orbital device.

2. Space Station, Ultra Station, Death Star - Stations are expensive to manufacture, but provide maximum functionality in orbit: the construction of any ships and the use of two orbital devices.

IT influence

The gates installed on the station are scanners for the gates of other planets. If there is a gate on an IT planet with a jump radius of N ly, and at a distance of M ly there is an enemy planet with a gate, and the station itself has a cloak of C%, then it will be scanned if the condition is met:

N >= M/(1-C)

This scanning means that IT knows information about the planet (ships in orbit are not scanned).

ISB influence

When you select ISB, 2 designs become available: “Space Dock” and “Ultra Station”. Otherwise it will be impossible to use them.

If ISB is selected and non-AR is selected, then all orbital stations become 20% cheaper to produce (cost in resources and minerals). This rule applies to both the cost of cases and the cost of components (that is, components will cost 40% of the original cost).

If ISB is selected, then the initial cloak value of +20% is set to all orbital stations (this will be 20% for non-SS races and 80% for SS races, respectively).
