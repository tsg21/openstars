Planetary Defenses

Planetary defense is a set of stationary planetary structures designed to protect the population and buildings from enemy attacks - bombings, mineral packages and landings.

For AR there is no concept of planetary protection.

Defenses at any given time have two characteristics: the number of buildings and their type.

The number of defenses can be from 0 to 100 on the planet, the type is determined in accordance with the development of technology in the direction of Energy. Defenses can be built, and in the future they will be automatically upgraded when new levels of technology are opened.

Defensive structures have a standard cost of 15 resources, 5 kt I, 5 kt B, 5 kt G per building (for IS the cost will be 9 resources, 3 kt I, 3 kt B, 3 kt G per building).

Defenses are built by the colony and can be destroyed in the event of bombing or the arrival of a mineral package. Also, if colonists leave the planet or the planet is captured by representatives of another race, the defenses are destroyed.

The number of defenses can be built according to the number that the colonists can control for the current climate conditions. This number is 10 or 100. 10 if the planet has a negative Value (unsuitable for reproduction at the moment), 100 if the planet is green and can theoretically accommodate 247,600 colonists - this is exactly how many colonists are needed to support 100 defenses.

The effectiveness of defenses is determined based on the number of controlled protective structures and their type. Efficiency is expressed in the ability to protect the planet from possible attacks. For defenses, this is a percentage that shows how many attacking weapons will be neutralized. This percentage has a standard value for standard and cluster bomb attacks and a reduced value for smart bombs. For airborne attacks the calculation is much more complex.

When viewing information about a planet that is not being scanned, Stars! show the predicted % protection. This is not an exact value, but it is constant for a certain number of protections of a certain type.
