Description

Mini Bomber
The fastest obtainable, lightest and cheapest bomber. Due to these characteristics, it is most preferred in the game. Like any bomber, it flies very low and close due to its small fuel tank.

B-17 Bomber
Medium class bomber. If desired, it can fit into gates, has a special expansion slot, and only due to this can it be better used compared to its younger brother.

Stealth Bomber
Bomber of the SS race, will not be available to others. It has expanded slots for Elect, which allows you to make a ship with maximum stealth. Indispensable for covert attacks in deep space.

B-52 Bomber
The heaviest bomber. Due to its mass, it is effective only in local shock conflicts or under the control of the IT race.
