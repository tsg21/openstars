Description

Midget Miner
Starting miner for all races that choose ARM. Will not be available without ARM. Cheap and light, therefore most applicable.

Mini Miner
The only miner for OBRM races. Available to all races. Compared to other miners it is expensive and heavy.

Miner
Medium ship for digging. Only available to races with ARM.

Maxi-Miner
Available to races without OBRM. For non-ARM races, the largest ship for digging.

Ultra Miner
The biggest miner in the game. Only available to races with ARM.
