Description

Scout
Base ship for reconnaissance. Cheap to produce, does not require high levels of science. Therefore, it is widely used at the initial stage of the game. In the middle and final phase of the game it can be used as a chaff.

Frigate
Designed as an advanced reconnaissance ship, this hull has a wide range of functionality. Can be used as a chaff, a convenient hull for minelayers, a good combat beam ship at the initial stage of wars (before the advent of missile-class weapons).

Comparison of Scout and Frigate hulls

Frigate enters the game a little later than Scout and immediately begins to compete with him on equal terms.

In reconnaissance tasks, Frigate is superior to Scout in quality parameters. With an almost equal cost of the case (and in some cases, with further play, a cheaper case) it has a larger fuel tank capacity. In addition, Frigate can accommodate more scanners, which increases their effective radius. With all this, with the help of the Frigate hull it is possible to create a scout with high maneuverability in battle, which makes destroying the scout almost impossible at the initial stage of the game.

As a chaff, the Frigate is a less flavorful chaff, but has a larger fuel tank and may be cheaper, which may make the difference with this design.
