All case types have a mediocre basic "Armor". Such Armor does not have a decisive impact and is sufficient with almost zero construction.

The base initiative for all types of orbital stations is quite high. At the initial period of development, it is enough to hinder enemy ships; in the subsequent game, new types of starbases enter and due to this they become a more serious obstacle due to the presence of Elec slots, which in turn also allows you to vary the initiative with the defending fleet.

Description

Orbital Fort
The fort is used for basic protection of planets from possible attacks. It is cheap to produce - can be built in most cases in 1 year - there are enough minerals from dismantling the colonization module, 32 or 40 resources are enough, which is quite importable. At the same time, it has the necessary capacity to repel an attack in the initial period of the game.

Space Dock
This station is the most powerful tool in rapid expansion. It is built very quickly (and requires a small number of minerals), is capable of refueling other ships (the most important element for expansion), can build small ships (which is quite enough for the initial and in some cases the middle period of the game), and can be used for basic protection of planets from possible attacks. Unlike the fort, it is already effective in missile defense, as it has 4 Elec slots.

Space Station
Base station, which is given at the start of the game. The cheapest design that has two orbital slots and can produce all types of ships. Effective in defense (1.5 times stronger than “Space Dock”). Refusal from it may be due to the need for more powerful protection (Ultra Station) or the loss of one of the sciences (for example, the presence of Bio 4 and Construction more than 16), which makes it more expensive than “Ultra Station”.

Ultra Station
A more powerful version of "Space Station". For combat slots ~ 1.5 times more effective.

Death Star
The maximum level of development of orbital stations. Can only be built by the AR race.
