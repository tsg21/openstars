Description

Small Freighter
The initial transport for all races. Has an average cost to produce. Used only at the initial stage of the game in the absence of larger scale transports. This is due to the fact that the development of new design technologies is sufficient using only the home planet, and as a result, this option is cheaper than direct use. After mastering the design, the “Medium Freighter” is practically not used.

Medium Freighter
A ship for the initial transport of colonists and cargo. Its main competitor is the Privateer, which does not require a G and can be configured with a higher fuel capacity.

Large Freighter
The main ship to perform transport functions until the end of the game after it is mastered. Can only be replaced for specific transport operations by ships such as the Rogue and Galeon.

Super Freighter
The heaviest vehicle in the game. Can only be used by the IS race. Has a larger number of slots for protecting or hiding from the enemy.
