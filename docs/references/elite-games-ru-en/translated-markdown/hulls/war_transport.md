Description

Privateer
As planned, it is a vehicle capable of fighting. In practice, it turned out that a loaded transport ceases to be a significant combat unit against pure warships, plus it is simply weaker due to its high cost and the lack of possible slots for improved combat characteristics. As a result, it is well used as a simple transport. After mastering the design, the “Large Freighter” is practically not used.

Rogue
Quite a multifunctional case. It is best used for its intended purpose (piracy), for which there are combat slots, expanded Elect for embedding invisibility devices, and an expanded fuel tank (compared to ships of the same level). Can be used as a combat missile ship (Elect slots for Computers), as a secretive minelayer, as a hider for other ships.

Galeon
The most powerful combat vehicle. Rarely used, since classic transports are more suitable for transportation; it becomes too heavy for rocket ships. For hiding other ships, it is best for all non-SS races.
