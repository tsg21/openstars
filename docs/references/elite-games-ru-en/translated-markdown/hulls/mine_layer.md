Description

Mini Mine Layer
Cheap minelayer. Available only to SD races. Does not take damage from mine detonations. It is cheap to produce and can easily penetrate gates with mines, which is why it is most preferable.

Super Mine Layer
The largest minelayer. Available only to SD races. Does not take damage from mine detonations. The large mass of the ship makes it difficult to use. But the presence of a large number of additional slots allows it to be used for a number of exceptional tasks.
