Order of Events

During the generation of a turn, all events in the game occur in the strict order described below. Before the first event, orders are distributed, the events themselves occur during generation, and after the last one the player can already see the consequences of the move.

1. Scrapping of ships in orbit. In this case, it is possible to obtain technologies.

2. Load at point wp0 (done manually via the Xfer button).

3. Unloading at point wp0.

4. At point wp0, colonization/landing battles on the planet (with possible technology transfer).

5. Loading at point wp0, made through an order (load).

6. Execution of other orders at point wp0.

7. Moving MT.

8. Mineral packets in outer space move and reduce their mass (or disintegrate).

8.a. PP packets terraform planets when hit.

8.b. Packages cause damage (destroying colonists and defenses).

8.c. Planets that lose population due to packages become uninhabitable (lose orbital stations, defense structures and scanner).

9. Entry points of spatial holes (Wormhole) move.

10. The ships are moving (it is possible to stop due to lack of fuel, fuel is taken by rams, getting into minefields (with damage and loss of speed), gate movement, jumping through a spatial hole (Wormhole)).

11. IS colonists grow in holds (and if there are more of them than in the hold, and they are above their planet, they will land).

12. Salvage is destroyed.

13. The exit points of spatial holes (Wormhole) move.

14. The output points of spatial holes (Wormhole) disappear or switch to each other.

15. SD minefields explode (possible re-damage to ships that moved in the field this year).

16. Mining of minerals.

17. Random events.

17.a. Moves and changes the trajectory of the MT.

17.b. Comets are falling.

17.s. The climate of the planets is changing.

17.d. Discovery of new isotopes of elements.

17.e. The emergence of a new MT.

18. Manufacturing (including science, launching packages, creating ships and orbital stations).

19. Espionage in science SS.

20. The population of the planets is growing or dying.

21. The mineral packets that were launched this year move and end up on the receiving planet.

22. Refueling ships from starbases.

23. Battles occur (with possible technology transfer).

24. Meeting with MT.

25. Bombing.

25.a. Calculation of damage to the first player.

25.b. Causing damage - destruction of buildings, destruction of the population.

25.c. Repeat points a, b for the remaining players in order (2, 3, 4, ..).

26. Planets with zero population become uninhabitable, with the loss of orbital stations and defensive structures.

27. Unloading of goods at point wp1.

28. Colonization and ground battles at wp1 (with possible technology transfer).

29. Loading at point wp1.

30. Installation of minefields.

31. Transfer of ships to other races (fleet transfer).

32. Consolidation of fleets at point wp1 (Merge order).

33. CA terraforms planets under its control.

34. Minefields are decreasing due to natural reasons.

35. Mines are cleared with beam weapons.

36. Repair of ships and orbital stations.

37. Remote terraforming.
