Ships

Ships are the main functional units in the game. With their help, colonization of other planets, transportation of goods and colonists, attack and defense of other ships, reconnaissance, bombing of planets, installation and clearing of minefields, remote mining of minerals, repair of other ships, etc. are carried out. At any time, the ship belongs to one of the players and during the game can be transferred to another player. Each ship has its own design that determines its characteristics.

Ships can only be built on planets with starbases. Starbases of the Space Station, Ultra Station, and Death Star classes can build any ships; on the Space Dock starbase you can build ships with a tonnage of up to 200 kt; Orbital Fort does not allow you to build ships. In addition, all starbases, except Orbital Fort, refuel ships.
