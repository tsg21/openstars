Galaxy

The galaxy is the general map of the game, on which the planets and the open space surrounding them are already located. In the game, the galaxy may be called Universe.

The galaxy is a square field, presented in the form of a grid, at the nodes of which planets and ships can be located. The ship must always be in a certain integer coordinate, and accordingly, the ships meet at this point.

The size of the galaxy and the number of planets are indicated at the very beginning of the game, when creating a party.
