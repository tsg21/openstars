Stars! ship classification

Report classification

In Stars! there is its own internal classification, which affects various moments in the game. According to this classification, ships are divided into the following types:

Scout

All ships of the Frigate, Scout, Destroyer hulls, regardless of weapons and components.

Unarmed

It is implied that these are unarmed ships. These include the * Freighter, * Miner, Super Fuel Xport, Fuel Transport and * Colony Ship hulls.

Bomber

Ships capable of carrying bombs: * Bomber.

Warship

All ships that do not fall into the groups described above.

These types of ships are shown when describing the report on past battles (Reports→Battle...)

Battle classification

The classification is based on the combat characteristics of the ships:

Freighters

Only "*Freighter" and "Colony Ship" hulls.

Fuel Transports

"Super Fuel Xport" and "Fuel Transport" hulls only.

Bombers

Ships capable of carrying bombs: "* Bomber".

Starbase

All orbital stations, including the fort.

Unarmed

All unarmed ships.

Armed

All ships armed with at least one unit of military weapons.

Any

Any ships.

When drawing up battle plans, you can select targets only from the specified groups.

Please note that "Miner" type cases are included only in the Unarmed and Any groups, respectively.

General classification of buildings

Stars! is designed in such a way that the game has a number of technological lines, when a certain type of component/case/technology appears and is improved with the development of technology. On this basis in Stars! Ship hulls can be divided into several lines:

Mini-colony Ship, Colony Ship

Ships intended for colonization. There is space for a colonial module and engine - as much as you don’t mind losing when colonizing the planet.

Scout, Frigate

Intelligence ships. Cheap body, scanner slots, no armor and no significant slots.

Small Freighter, Medium Freighter, Large Freighter, Super Freighter

Transports. They don’t know how to do anything except transport cargo and colonists. But they do it effectively.

Privateer, Rogue, Galleon

Combat transports. They have a basic carrying capacity and a certain number of slots for weapons or increased functionality.

Destroyer, Cruiser, Battle Cruiser

Combat escort ships. Mandatory weapons and no significant component weapon amplifiers.

Battleship, Dreadnought

Battleships. Focus on powerful weapons and strong armor.

Fuel Transport, Super-Fuel Transport

Engineering ships. Ship repairs and refueling.

Midget Miner, Mini-Miner, Miner, Maxi-Miner, Ultra-Miner

Miners. Their occupation is mineral extraction.

Mini Mine Layer, Super Mine Layer

Minelayers. They lay mines well and do not suffer from the detonation of minefields.

Mini Bomber, B-17 Bomber, Stealth Bomber, B-52 Bomber

Bombers. Only they can carry bombs (with the exception of the Multi Contained Munition device from MT).

Mini Morph, Meta Morph, Nubian

Multifunctional housings. Can be used to perform almost any task.

Capital and Escort ships

These classes include all warships (ships with at least one laser, missile/torpedo on board) or bombs and, as a result, have a positive combat rating (Rating). Depending on its meaning, such ships can be either escort (Escort) or main (Capital).

All ships that have a Rating of 2000 or more are considered Capital. All others with a positive Rating are Escort ships. With zero rating - Unarmed.
