Ground combat can involve an amphibious attack, a ship-based bombardment of a planet, or a simultaneous colonization battle.

All of these actions attempt to change the population of the enemy planet through various means.

Landing

The landing represents the unloading of colonists onto an enemy planet. This action is performed in the same way as unloading cargo.

The landing may be hindered by the orbital station. That is, if it is present, then the landing will not be carried out. In the case of an “Unload” order, the order will be cancelled. When unloading through the “XFer” button, all paratroopers will die before reaching the surface of the planet.

Bombing

The bombing of planets is carried out by ships that must be in orbit of the planet. If bombers (here: these are the ships that carry bombs) are in orbit, then they are trying to bomb anyway. The only thing that can prevent this activity is an orbital station that protects the planet from all types of bombs (regular, smart, terraforming).

Accordingly, to stop the bombing, it is necessary to remove the bombers from orbit.

To protect against bombing, you need to either destroy ships with bombs or leave the orbital station intact.

Simultaneous colonization

If several races simultaneously try to colonize at one step, then a battle for the planet occurs.

During the battle, the following calculation of the winner occurs.

1) Stars! They are searching for who has the largest population for colonization. If there are 2 or more such races, then no one gets the planet.

2) The found race occupies the planet.

3) This race suffers losses equal to the largest population that participated in the battle for colonization among races that have a number lower than this one. For example, if this is the first race, then it colonizes without loss. If it is the second, then it has a minus of the first. If it is the third, then the greatest of the first and second is taken away from it.
