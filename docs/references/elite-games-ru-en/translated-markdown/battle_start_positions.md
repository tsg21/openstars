Starting positions

At the beginning of the battle, all fleets are in one of the cells. Ships of the same race are in the same cell, and the position of this cell depends on how many races are participating in the battle and what kind of race this race is.

If N races participate in the battle, then the relative initial disposition of their fleets will be the same and correspond to one of the schemes:

Each diagram shows N different races. Which race is numbered is determined by their order in the race arrangement in the game. For example, if there are 6 races in the game in numerical order A, B, C, D, E, F, and races B, F, D, A are participating in the battle, then there will be a starting position of the field with 4 races, with A in the first position, B in the second, D in the third, and F in the fourth.

Sometimes the number of races involved in a battle is critical. With a large number of races, positions are possible when the main warships of the battle are very close to each other or, for example, there is a rapprochement with an orbital station. The presence of an extra allied ship most often radically changes the initial alignment, which can be used.
