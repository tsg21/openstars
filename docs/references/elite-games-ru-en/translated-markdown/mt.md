Mystery Trader (MT)

MT is an independent entity. It moves at speeds from 7 to 13, and over time it can change both direction and speed.

Its appearance in the galaxy becomes known to everyone and the MT is visible regardless of its presence or coverage by a scanner.

This is a turquoise colored object that is indestructible and can only be encountered. Each race can meet one MT once. It involves a fleet with a cargo of 5 Mt or more minerals on board at the same point as the MT. In this case, MT takes the entire fleet for itself (the fleet disappears forever) and gives in return one of:

1. A unique on-board component, housing or device.

2. One or more technological levels in science.

3. Several unique ships of the same type.

Any of the above is a good purchase and, if possible, it is highly recommended to intercept MT.

MT is not a fleet, so you can catch up with it, but you only need to follow it next to it by accurately calculating the trajectory of its movement.

The MT sometimes moves at a speed higher than the maximum speed, so it can be impossible to catch up with it and interception is possible only at a separate point in space where it is during its move - these points are marked by risks on the straight trajectory (when choosing the MT, see figure).

The MT makes changes in speed and direction arbitrarily. When changing, it either increases the speed by 1, or decreases it by 1, or does not change if the speed is 7 (with a 50% probability, i.e., with an “attempt” to reduce it to 6) or if the speed is 13 (with a similar “attempt” to increase the speed to 14). This action occurs with a probability of 2 to 10% (depending on the size of the galaxy, 2 for Huge, 10 for Tiny).

MT is taken by the fleet, and only one. Many ships carrying minerals for MT must be combined into one fleet.

MT tries to give the same thing to all races. If he gives technology to a race, then he will also give technology to another race that organizes a meeting with him. If a MT gives a unique device, body or component, then he will give this thing to all races. If the race already has this item, then MT will try to give another. If you have a full set of technologies or a full set of unique things, then it may turn out that the MT will not give anything at all, but the fleet will take it for itself.

The game practices “guarding” the MT so that enemy races cannot meet with it. This is done by destroying transports going to meet the MT. During a turn, the meeting with the MT occurs after the battle, so interception of other people's transports can be carried out at the point where the MT is located.

MT can give one of the devices:

1. Mini Morph.

2. Mega Poly Shell.

3.Multi Contained Munition.

4. Hush-a-Boom.

5.Multi Function Pod.

6. Enigma Pulsar.

7. Multi Cargo Pod.

8. Jump Gate.

9. Alien Miner.

10. Langston Shell.

11. Anti Matter Torpedo.

12. Genesis Device.

Any item on the list is not available until given to MT. Further, when the required level of technology is achieved, it can be used.

Genesis Device is a planetary device (built on a planet). Upon receiving it, the player can build this device on his planets and after construction the planet is reborn. Rebirth means that all climatic parameters and mineral concentrations change randomly (as at the start of the game). The device does not affect the HW property (the planet changes, but remains HW) and minerals on the surface of the planet do not disappear anywhere.

MT can give several (from 1 to 4 usually) ships of one of the following types:

Each of these ships does not represent a significant force, therefore it is used for targeted purposes, such as, for example, a scout with a green scanner in the absence of scanners due to NAS, can be disassembled, as a result of which a technology or a unique device (which is present on board or the hull) can be obtained, but this is already with a probability of about 2% for disassembly.

If it is unknown what the MT gives, or it is already known that he gives ships, it is recommended to clear the design before meeting him, otherwise you will not receive the ships.

The last thing MT can give is levels in technology. This can be 1 or more levels. If you have just started the game, you can get 6-7 levels, if you have already advanced far in learning technologies, you can only get 1 level. At the same time, MT does not give levels, but resources, so after receiving technologies, look to see if the distance to the next levels has changed in the technology branches.
