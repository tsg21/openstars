Design of ships and stations

All design takes place in the “Commands” → “Ship Design” menu.

Here you can design stations and ships, as well as manage existing designs.

Case and slot concept

In a game, to create a new design, it is necessary to use a certain known body. At the beginning of the game, a certain set of buildings is given based on the level of technology development, and in the future this list may expand as a result of raising the “Construction” level.

After selecting a case, a copy is made of it and the corresponding elements can be added to the existing slots of the case.

Each slot has a type, which determines the components that can be inserted into it, and a capacity, which determines how many components the slot can hold. One slot can accommodate up to a specified number of components of the same type.

When inserting a component, the total cost of the design increases by the corresponding number of resources and minerals, and when designing a ship, its mass increases.

Component groups

All currently available components are shown in the corresponding list. From the designer's point of view, they are divided into groups:

* All (all components)

* Armor

* Beam Weapons

*Bombs

* Electrical

* Engines

*Mechanical

*Mine Layers

* Mining Robots

*Orbital

*Scanners

*Shields

*Torpedoes

* Weapons

The “Weapons” group includes all the “Torpedoes” and “Beam Weapons” components, otherwise the components do not overlap (they are in one of the groups).

The “Orbital” group is available only when designing orbital stations. The “Bombs”, “Engines”, “Mechanical”, “Mine Layers”, “Mining Robots” and “Scanners” groups are only available when designing ships.

Slot types

The following types of slots exist:

Components of the "Engines" group. Available on all ships in a single copy and must be completely filled with an engine of the same type.

General purpose slot. Available only on ships. It is possible to use component groups: “Armor”, “Weapons”, “Electrical”, “Mechanical”, “Mine Layers”, “Scanners” and “Shields”. Accordingly, it is impossible to use “Engines” and “Mining Robots”.
