Planetary scanners

Planetary scanners are unique structures for a planet that scan nearby open space and planets. There may be only one scanner of this type on the planet or there may not be one at all.

The cost of planetary scanners is constant - 100 resources, 10 kt I, 10 kt B, 70 kt G.

With the development of technology, planetary scanners are upgraded automatically, without any resource costs.

If members of a race leave the planet or the planet is captured, the scanner is destroyed. Thus, it is impossible to directly determine the presence of a scanner in any way, only by indirect signs.

The Planetary Scanner is not destroyed during any type of bombing.

If NAS is selected, planetary scanners have a double scanning radius.
