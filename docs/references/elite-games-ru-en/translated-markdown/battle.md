Battles

Battles are events that take place in outer space or in planetary orbit as a result of armed conflicts between races.

If at one point in the galaxy (with the same integer coordinates) there are many ships and possibly an orbital station, and at the same time one of the ships is combat (has on board a beam weapon or at least one missile or torpedo) and has a battle plan to defeat other ships, then a battle takes place in which all ships located at this point participate. If there are more than 256 ships at a point, then pay attention to the bug.

There can only be one battle at one point. The surviving ships and/or orbital station remain at the battle point.
