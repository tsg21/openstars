Beam Weapons

The components are designed to forcefully destroy enemy ships in close combat.

All weapon components are located in the “Beam Weapons” section of the component classification.

There are four lines according to the classification of types of beam weapons. In addition to them, there are also special types of beam weapons, which will be discussed separately.

In any laser weapon, at zero distance, basic damage is inflicted on the enemy, and with distance the damage weakens (quite slightly). In this case, beam weapons always hit shields first, and only then can they cause damage to the body (except for sappers, which only hit shields).

All beam weapons can be divided into point and mass. Mass components include all components except the Gatling systems.

In the case of point weapons, one volley of lasers hits one target. If a salvo is able to destroy one target, then it destroys it and in the same salvo redirects the remaining power to other targets within the destruction radius.

In the case of gatling systems, a single salvo fires a pinpoint laser shot at all groups of ships within the affected radius.

Starting weapon

This line includes three types of lasers that are in their early stages of development. All of them have a damage radius of 1 cell and differ only in combat power and cost.

Short range weapons

All components of this type have a damage radius of 0, that is, you can hit the enemy only if the ship is in the same cell as the one firing (orbital stations, respectively, on 1 cell).

Perhaps this weapon was a strategic mistake by the creators, since it did not find a worthy use in the game. The weapon is powerful, but at the same time it is almost impossible for them to hit the target, since it will definitely leave the attacker’s cell. The weapon is heavy, which makes it difficult to maneuver in battle and makes it much more difficult to hit the exact position of the enemy.

The weapon itself is so powerful that it is one and a half times more powerful even in comparison with the next generation of lasers.

Requires an extremely large amount of B and an average amount of resources.
