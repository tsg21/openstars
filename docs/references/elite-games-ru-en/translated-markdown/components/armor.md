Armor

Components increase the armor component of a ship or station (Hull).

Each ship or station has a base Hull value, and installing armor components allows you to increase this value.

All armor components are located in the “Armor” section of the component classification.

There are two lines according to the classification of armor types. In addition to them, there are also special types of armor, which will be discussed separately.

Structural armor

This type of armor is mastered as a result of mastering science levels in the “Construction” direction. This type of armor has a large number of types that appear at all stages of the game, and therefore is more relevant. In addition, this armor has a large mass and requires mainly mineral I to produce.

Bio armor

This type of armor is used when reaching levels in Bio. This is lighter armor and is therefore well used on small ships. The downside is the cap at 175 dp and possible early game G deficit, which is what it's made of.
