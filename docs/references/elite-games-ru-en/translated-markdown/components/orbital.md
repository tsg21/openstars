Orbital devices

Orbital devices can only be installed on orbital stations.

All orbital devices are divided into two groups: Gates and Mass Drivers.

Gates

Gates are designed for fast movement of ships and cargo between planets (cargo for IT only). Each gate has two parameters: mass and distance. If the moved mass fits into the mass and distance limits for both the receiver and the source, then the movement occurs without problems. If at least one limit is exceeded, then the ship will receive damage, and there will be a possibility of annihilation (loss of a unit along with the cargo) when jumping. Theoretically, gates can propel a ship 5 times heavier or 5 times its nominal distance.

HE races cannot build gates.

Batch engines

Packet engines allow the formation of mineral packets that move from planet to planet at high speeds. If there is another packet engine on the receiving planet that can accept the packet at running speed, then it accepts and the minerals can be made available to the colony. Otherwise, such a package kills some or all of the colonists, and can also change the climatic conditions of the planet if the package was from PP.
