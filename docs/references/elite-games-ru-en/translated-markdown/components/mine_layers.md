Mine Layers

Minefield laying devices are designed to create minefields or enrich them with new mines, which only happens with the order “Lay Minefield”.

There are three types of mines in total, according to which they are grouped in this section.

Standard mines

The only type of mine that can be used by all types of races.

They differ in stopping and basic damage to the fleet if the latter is blown up in a minefield of this type.

Booby traps

These mines impede rapid advancement. In this case, they do not cause damage, but have a much lower safe speed.

They are distinguished by their large mass and cost in resources and I.
