Engines

A necessary component for ships. Each ship can use one of the engine types and occupy the entire engine slot.

Engines are classified into three groups: standard, trans engines and ramscoop.

Fuel consumption during flight in space, as well as maneuvering speed in combat, depend on the characteristics of the engine. Depending on the type, the engine may provide a safe field at the maximum 10th speed or not (if not, then moving at that speed can lead to the destruction of the entire ship).

Standard engines

Basic type of engines. They are used in the early stages of the game and in designs that do not require high speed or movement at the required low cost.

Standard engines have a characteristic dependence of fuel consumption on speed, for which three sections are distinguished: 1) low consumption at low speeds 2) standard consumption 3) ultra-high consumption.

Most often, such ships fly at the maximum speed of standard consumption. It is almost impossible to fly in the third section, since fuel consumption is an order of magnitude higher than standard. In this regard, ships are more often built that are not capable of sudden fast jumps, that have a measured flight speed between planets and at the same time good maneuverability. Sometimes the low weight of the engine (compared to trans engines) is important.

Standard engines are unable to receive fuel from space. This only happens at flight speed 1 (the slowest) - in this case, the ship is effectively a stationary target.

In terms of costs, they require mainly I mineral and an average number of resources.

Ramscoop class engines

Engines of this class differ from others primarily in that they allow the generation of fuel in outer space while moving at a certain speed. In addition, these engines are in some cases lighter and cheaper, used in the middle and final stages of the game (in large numbers at the initial stage of Fuel Mizer), and are more susceptible to explosions in minefields.
