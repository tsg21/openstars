Mechanical

Mechanics are designed to perform a number of different functions and are divided into groups according to them.

All mechanical devices can only be installed on ships.

In total, there are four line-groups based on functional purpose and separate single devices.

Colonization modules

These devices are designed for the colonization of uninhabited planets. Available at the beginning of the game..

Only with a device of this type can the “Colonize” order be executed, as a result of which the entire fleet along with the colonization module will be dismantled.

Beam weapon deflector

This device weakens the effect of enemy rays by 10%. This property is additive, that is, 2 deflectors will protect, weakening the beam attack to (1 - 0.1) * (1 - 0.1) = 81% of the nominal value.
