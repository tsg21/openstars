Electronics

Electronics are designed to perform a number of different functions and are divided into groups according to them.

In total, there are four line-groups based on functional purpose and separate single devices.

Concealment devices

These devices are designed to hide a ship or orbital station from enemy scanners. They are mastered as a result of mastering the levels of science in the direction of “Electronics” and, to a lesser extent, “Energy”.

Missile and torpedo guidance devices

Commonly called computers.

These devices increase the chance of torpedoes and missiles (launched by ships or orbital stations on which they are installed) to hit the target.

In addition, these devices increase the initiative of all combat devices (torpedoes, missiles, beam weapons), which can also be used effectively.

They are mastered as a result of mastering the levels of science in the direction of “Electronics” and, to a lesser extent, “Energy”.

Missile and torpedo deflection devices

These devices reduce the chance of torpedoes and missiles (launched at ships or orbital stations on which they are installed) hitting the target.

They are mastered as a result of mastering the levels of science in the direction of “Electronics” and, to a lesser extent, “Energy”.

They are used quite rarely, since in combat conditions the use of chaff is more economical. It fits well into orbital stations, since it is impossible to protect them with chaff.

Beam weapon amplifiers

Also called capacitors.

The devices are designed to enhance the power of the beam weapons of the ship or orbital station on which they are installed.

The maximum beam weapon can be enhanced by 2.5 times, despite the fact that the enhancement is additive (the second device enhances the first).

They are mastered as a result of mastering the levels of science in the direction of “Energy” and, to a lesser extent, “Electronics”.

Retarder in battle

A special device that slows down all (both your own and others’) moving units in battle by 1 cell per turn. This allows you to slow down the attack of any type of ship or slow down the escape of those shot down during a hunt.

The device is either present in battle (slows down by 1) or absent.

Can only be built by the SD race.
