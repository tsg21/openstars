Bombs

The components are designed to bomb enemy planets in order to destroy the enemy's economy and population or worsen planetary living conditions.

There are three lines according to the classification of bomb types. In addition to them, there are also two special types of bombs, which will be discussed separately.

Regular bombs

This type of bomb is designed to destroy both the population and enemy planetary structures. This is the most commonly used type in the game. Bomb manufacturing technologies are obtained as a result of mastering the levels of science in the “Weapons” direction. In addition, these bombs use a small number of resources and a large amount of mineral B.

Cluster bombs

This type of bomb is designed to destroy enemy planetary structures. In the game it is used to quickly and cheaply suppress planetary defenses. Bomb manufacturing technologies are obtained as a result of mastering the levels of science in the direction of “Electronics” and to a lesser extent “Weapons”. In addition, these bombs use a small number of resources and an average amount of minerals B and G. Bombs have less mass than other types, and therefore can be used both as a complex and in separate light groups.

Smart bombs

In the game they are called "Smart" class bombs.

This type of bomb is designed to destroy enemy populations while preserving planetary structures. In the game it is used to capture enemy planets along with industrial capacities. Bomb manufacturing technologies are obtained as a result of mastering levels of science in the direction of “Biotechnology” and, to a lesser extent, “Weapons”. In addition, these bombs use a large number of resources and an average amount of B minerals.

Protection against these bombs is weaker, but bombs have a % limit, regardless of the bomber fleet.

Most often, this type of bomb is considered useless by players due to the limited percentage of penetration of planetary defenses, and because of this, in a protracted war, the siege of planets without suppressing the defense becomes impossible.

All bombs of this class are prohibited from use by IS races.
