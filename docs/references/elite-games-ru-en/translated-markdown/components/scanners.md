Scanners

Scanners are designed to scan space, as well as the orbits and surfaces of planets.

In total, there are four groups of scanners: regular, penetrating and pirate.

Conventional scanners are divided into two lines - electrical and bio-scanners.

Regular scanners

Conventional scanners allow you to scan outer space and the planet in orbit of which the scanner is located. In the interface, scanning is shown with a red background.

Penetration scanners

Penetrating scanners (or advanced) can scan the orbits and surfaces of planets from a distance. When using them, the ship has two scanning radii - the radius of a regular scanner and the radius of a penetrating one.

These scanners (except Chameleon Scanner) are available to all races who did not choose NAS.
