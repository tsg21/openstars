Mining Robots

The robots are designed for remote digging, that is, mining minerals on the planet at a time when there are no colonists on it.

There are also types of robots for changing the planet's climate.

All robotic miners are combined into one line-group and two special types can be considered separately.

Robot miners

Designed for remote digging, can be used by all PRT race types.

To use, science levels in the direction of “Construction” and to a lesser extent “Electronics” are required.
