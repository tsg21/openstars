Torpedoes & Missiles

Torpedoes and missiles are long-range weapons. This is a ranged attack that needs to be used wisely and from which you need to defend wisely.

Unlike lasers, torpedoes and missiles always have a longer range. In addition, torpedoes or missiles hit the hull of a ship if there are shields on it (if they hit, half the damage goes to the armor, and the other half to the shields).

All torpedoes and missiles require a small amount of resources to produce, but a large amount of the mineral I. This allows you to quickly build defenses on planets, but makes it difficult to massively use torpedoes and missiles.

The evolution of torpedoes and missiles occurs in jumps once every ~ 4 levels. This event completely cancels the previous generation, since missiles and torpedoes are strengthened ~2 times, and in some cases the range increases.

Torpedoes

Torpedoes are used and developed at all stages of the game. At the initial stage, torpedoes are weak and heavy, but usable. And after the advent of missiles, they have very limited use.

Rockets

Compared to missiles, torpedoes are lighter and have better accuracy, which does not require computers. Torpedoes can be used at all stages of the game. Also, torpedoes are more indifferent to chaff.

Missiles, in turn, have advantages that make the use of torpedoes a rare occurrence. This is primarily a large striking force and a long range. At the same time, missiles have the property of causing twice as much damage if their target does not have shields.
