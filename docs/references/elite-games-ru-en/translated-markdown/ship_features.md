Ship characteristics set

Cost in resources and minerals

The amount of resources (Resource) and minerals (Ironium, Boranium, Germanium) that must be spent during the construction process.

Ship type

It is determined by the Hull Type and in some cases depends on the set of components. See classification.

Mass

Mass of the ship in kt. Determines the possibility of getting through the gates and fuel consumption.

Load capacity (Cargo)

The amount of cargo in kt that the ship can transport.

Engine type

Determines what the fuel consumption or fuel supply will be for the corresponding speed, as well as the possibility of safe movement at the corresponding speed.

Fuel tank size (Max Fuel)

Measured in mg. Determines how much fuel the ship takes with it and, accordingly, how long the ship can go without refueling.

Armor

Measured in conventional units dp. Determines how stable the ship is in battle.

Shields

Measured in conventional units dp. Determines the level of shield strength.

Maneuverability in combat (Moves)

Determines how fast the ship can move during combat.

Rating

Ship's combat rating. Affects the status of the ship: Escort or Capital.

Initiative

Initiative in battle. Determines the firing order in battle.

Cloaking coefficient

Determines how invisible the ship will be to enemy scanners.

Torpedo/Missile Deflection Coefficient (Jam)

Determines how many torpedoes/missiles will be deflected when fired at this ship.

Scanner Range

Shows how far a given ship can scan space. There are 2 scanner parameters: normal (red, scans outer space) and penetrating (green, scans the orbits of planets and the planets themselves).

Laser attenuation coefficient (Beam Deflecting)

It is not stated explicitly anywhere. Determines how much a combat laser shot at a given ship will be weakened.

Combat parameters

Determined by the weapons present on board. Each weapon has its own range of damage, strength, initiative, division into slots - which affects the battle itself.

Min reserve

The number and type of mines a ship can lay per year.

Number of shafts to dig

The number of equivalent mines the ship uses to extract minerals.

Number of people destroyed during bombing

It is measured in percentage and in the minimum limit (based on the characteristics of the bombs).

Number of planetary structures destroyed during bombing

Measured in the number of buildings per year based on the characteristics of the bombs.

Other options

Other options, such as: recovery of mg of fuel per year (only for engineering ships), how much repairs of friendly ships can be done (only for engineering ships), the ability to rob ships/planets (if there are SS scanners), slowing down all ships in battle (a special device for SD races), the ability to colonize a planet (if there is a colonization module), the ability to gate in outer space (if there is an appropriate device), the ability and speed of terraforming planets (if there are OA robots or terra-bomb) and much more.
