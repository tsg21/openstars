Planetary climate

The climate is also called living conditions in the game, and in relation to a specific race - the hub.

Climate characteristics

At any given time, a planet is characterized by its gravity (Grav), temperature (Temp) and radiation (Rad).

Each of these parameters is a discrete value that can be in one of 101 values.

Radiation changes from 0 to 100 mR in 1 mR steps, temperature from -200 'C to +200 'C in 4 'C steps.

Climate change

The values of gravity, temperature and radiation are current and native. The native values ​​are the same as the current ones at the beginning of the game.

Current climate values can be changed within the limits set by the technological capabilities of terraforming using the following methods:

1. Carrying out terraforming (the colony spends a certain amount of resources).

2. Possible change when ingested by PP mineral pack.

3. Population of the planet by the CA race.

4. Liberation of the planet by the CA race.

5. Carrying out remote terraforming using OA.

6. Bombing with terraforming bombs.

Native climate values can be changed in the following cases:

1. Possible change when ingested by PP mineral pack.

2. Probable change of 1 point if the planet is inhabited by CA.

2. A random event - the fall of a comet, fundamental changes to the planet.

3. Construction of the Genesis Device.

Terraforming

Terraforming is the forced change of the planet’s climate by a certain race.

Terraforming can be carried out by any race according to its technological capabilities. They are determined based on the level of development of technology and the properties of the race itself.

The technological capabilities of terraforming represent the ability to change the planet's climate to a certain number of points from its original value.

Terraforming methods

By the forces of the colony

The CA race automatically, when populating or opening new levels in science, changes the planet's climate as much as possible, without spending resources. If CA leaves the planet or is captured, then the parameter values ​​​​return to their original values ​​automatically.

Other races must expend resources to carry out terraforming. TT races spend 70 resources to change the value by 1 point, non-TT races spend 100 resources. With this terraforming, Stars! They themselves determine which parameter change will have a more positive effect on the race. That is, if a change in temperature has a greater effect in increasing the % of the planet’s hub than gravity or radiation, then the temperature parameter will be changed by 1 point. When calculating the next terraforming by 1 point, the same choice is made (which may change). If the effect of the change is the same (in whole percent increase), then gravity has the highest priority, followed by temperature, and the least - radiation.

Remote terraforming

Remote terraforming is carried out using special robots - OA. They can only be built by CAs, but can be used by any race. To carry out this action, it is necessary to place a ship with a robot built into it into the orbit of the planet and it will carry out (if it can) terraforming automatically. If it is a friendly or friendly planet, then terraforming is carried out for the better; if it is an enemy, then for the worse.

Terraforming bombs can only terraform planets to their native value.

The scope of climate change by robots and bombs is limited by the technological development of the race to which they are used.

Terraforming with mineral packs

If a mineral package hits a planet and is not completely inhibited by the engines, then it has a chance to terraform the planet.

For such cases, a number of rules apply:

Climate change depends on the composition of the package

Ironium modifies Gravity.

Boranium changes Temperature

Germanium changes Radiation

Changes in native meanings do not depend on the level of technology development

Terraforming current values depends on development technology

Changes in current climate values occur as part of the technological development of the race that released the package.

Terraforming is always favorable for the race that sent the package

The change in climate values in the immune parameter is directed towards the nearby edge

Terraforming depends only on the mass of the package arriving on the planet

Doesn’t depend on the speed of the package, doesn’t depend on who is on the planet or not, or who stayed or not after the package landed.

Change in the native value of a planet on average 2 Mt by 1 point

Moreover, this value is random, that is, 2 Mt does not guarantee a change by 1 point, it may not change anything, or it may change by more than 1 point.

Changing the current value of the planet on average 200 kt by 1 point

This change is also random.

Climate change during the construction of the Genesis Device

When built, the planet receives random climate parameters.
