Interstellar Traveler

General description

Interstellar Traveler (IT) - a race that specializes in gating cargo, colonists and ships. It is easier for them to build gates, and the gates themselves are much more efficient. Gates allow you to move unlimited cargo and ship tonnage over unlimited distances.

Gating influences the method of expansion (colonization, construction of a gate and rapid transfer of colonists) and the capture of planets, and the design of warships, since in this case you don’t have to think about tonnage; and also on the distribution of minerals in large empires, when they can be balanced and quickly transferred.

The race is most attractive in large parties.

Benefits

2 starting planets

If the galaxy is larger than “Tiny”, then the race starts with 2 planets, one of which is HW.

Unique orbital gate designs

A unique set of gates available at different stages of the game, allowing you to ultimately move any tonnage to any distance.

Ability to gate loads

The race is allowed to move not only structures (as in other races), but also cargo in ships. In this case, the weight of the cargo is not taken into account.

Gates are cheaper to build

Gate construction is 25% cheaper.

Scanning planets with gates

If there is another planet with a gate and the IT gate reaches this planet, then it is scanned and visible to IT.

Reduced the likelihood of ships disappearing in gates

If the ship is not destroyed, then it is guaranteed to be gated (this is for the case of exceeding tonnage or distance).

Unique Component: Anti-Matter Generator

Contains fuel and is capable of generating fuel.

Disadvantages

Batch engines have been weakened

Packet engines are 2 times less efficient when receiving packets. In this case, all packages are destroyed in flight.

Quite an expensive race

This disadvantage is considered easily smoothed out against the background of all the advantages.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 scout.

2. 1 colonial ship.

3. 1 transport.

4. 1 destroyer.

Starting Science

* Level 5 in Propulsion.

* Level 5 in Construction.

PRT detection

High Prop at the beginning of the game

Non-IT races do not have a 5th Prop at the very beginning of the game, so they do not fly on the DLL7 engine.

Fast jumping capital ships

Transports move quickly. It is not noticeable that the transports are flying (stopped - they hang when gated).

Special gates at stations

Unique gates that appear quite quickly.

Notes

At the start, both planets have a basic gate. On one planet there is a Starbase, on another there is a fort or Space Dock.

Scanning of other planets is affected by their cloak. If a cloak is built into the scanned orbital station, then it has a corresponding effect on scanning IT planets (but nothing can save you from the all-seeing eye (a gate infinite in distance).

When scanning with gates, the fleet in orbit is not visible.
