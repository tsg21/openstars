Space Demolition

General description

Space Demolition (SD) - a race that specializes in mines and laying minefields, respectively. Mines are a defensive tool, and quite effective. At the same time, the race has protective properties from unique components, which gives it a unique defensive play style.

Benefits

Detonation of minefields

Has the ability to detonate its minefields, harming the enemy and its non-mine ships.

Ability to build booby traps

Mine setting is available using a range of Speed Trap devices.

Ability to build heavy mines

Mine placement is available using a range of Heavy Dispenser devices.

Safer movement through minefields

The race has 2 higher “safe speed” of movement through minefields.

Minefields have a scanning property

Scanning occurs as a non-penetrating scanner with a corresponding radius equal to the field radius.

Minefields decrease more slowly

Minefields when planets enter the field are reduced by 1% instead of 4% for other races.

Unique housing: Mini Mine Layer

Small and cheap minelayer. Knows how to lay mines at the arrival point. Other designs only carry out 1 move at the point.

Unique body: Super Mine Layer

The largest minelayer with built-in expansion slots. He also knows how to lay mines at the arrival point.

Unique component: Energy Dampener

Reduces the maneuverability of all ships in battle by 1.

Studying the design of enemy ships in a minefield

Design studies occur when an enemy ship is detonated in a specially created explosion or as a result of a high-speed hit in a minefield.

Unique components: Mine Dispenser 40, 80, 130

Standard mines that can explode remotely.

Unique components: Heavy Dispenser 50, 110, 200

Heavy mines, more throughput, but more damage.

Unique Components: Speed Trap 20, 30, 50

Mines do not cause damage, but can stop the advance of enemy fleets.

Disadvantages

No major drawbacks.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 combat scout.

2. 1 colonial ship.

3. 1 minelayer with conventional live mines.

4. 1 minelayer with speed reduction mines.

Starting Science

* Level 2 in Propulsion.

* Level 2 in Biotechnology.

PRT detection

Mines in the early stages

The race has destroyers already at the start and can pursue an attacking policy.

Laying mines upon arrival

Only minelayer corps can lay mines at point wp1.

Detonation of minefields

If the field belongs to a race and it is in it that the explosion definitely occurs. Remote detonation can be calculated based on the change in the number of mins.

Unique mines, components and hulls

For SD, these are original and effective designs.

Notes

Mine detonation does not affect orbital stations.

"Energy Dampener" works for both friendly and foreign ships.

Only standard mines explode remotely.
