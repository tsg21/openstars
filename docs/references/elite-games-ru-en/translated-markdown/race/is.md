Inner-Strength

General description

Inner-Strength (IS) - the race prefers defense to attack, ships are repaired faster, planets are better defended, and at the same time it is more difficult to pursue an aggressive policy.

Benefits

Colonists breed in ships

If you load colonists into a ship, they multiply at a rate of 1/2 the ideal conditions on the planet.

Colonists defend themselves better

If someone lands on your planet, then the colonists defend themselves 2 times more effectively than other races.

Ability to build booby traps

Mines can be set using the Speed Trap 20 device.

Faster repairs

Ships and orbital stations are repaired 1.5 times faster.

Cheaper planetary protection

Planetary protective installations cost 40% less.

Unique Hull: Super Freighter

The largest vehicle in terms of capacity in the game.

Unique Hull: Fuel Transport

An early modification of the Super-Fuel Transport tanker.

Unique weapon: Mini gun

A weapon for clearing mines and destroying large numbers of groups of ships.

Unique Component: Tachyon Detector

If the component is installed on a ship with a scanner, then it reduces the cloak of detected ships.

Unique armor: Fielded Kelarium

Armor with a built-in shield.

Unique Shield: Croby Sharmor

Shield with built-in armor.

Unique Component: Jammer 10

An early missile and torpedo evasion component.

Unique Component: Jammer 50

The most effective component for dodging missiles and torpedoes.

Disadvantages

Expensive weapons

All Weapons class components cost 25% more.

No smart bombs

The race is prohibited from building ships with Smart bombs.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 combat scout.

2. 1 colonial ship.

Starting Science

*All science is basic.

PRT detection

Unique components and housings

The race has a large number of unique components that will definitely be used.

Transports gain weight during flight

This happens when transporting colonists.

Well protected during landing

They are protected 2 times more effectively.

Expedited repair

Repairs go 1.5 times faster.

Using Speed Trap mines

Used already in the early stages of the game. Not to be confused with SD.

Notes

Colonists reproduce in transports at a speed of 1/2 of the given race. Moreover, they reproduce, rounding up every year with an accuracy of 1 kt, that is, out of 100 colonists it will never be possible to make 200.

With other races, it is possible to calculate the race reproduction rate. To do this, you need three points of free flight of transport, or 2 if you are sure that the ship is full of colonists.

If the hold is empty and the colonists are multiplying, then they jump to the planet over which the ship hangs. If the planet is alien or undeveloped, then no one jumps.

The race is a threat to SS, since when using the “Tachyon Detector” the SS becomes practically toothless and defenseless.

Croby Sharmor are great for frigates. “Fielded Kelarium” for the middle stage of playing armor-only slots.

Croby Sharmor is not affected by the RS nerf and shields are strengthened. On Fielded Kelarium, RS weakens armor and strengthens shield.

In flight, the star calculator, when taking into account Fuel, does not take into account that the colonists multiply and increase the weight of the fleet. Colonists reproduce after migration, and therefore this is relevant for migrations of more than one year.
