Scientific parameters of race

Scientific parameters are selected at the 6th step of creating a race.

These parameters affect how quickly a race is able to master new technologies through its own scientific research.

Technology areas

All technologies are divided into 6 areas, in each of which research can be carried out:

Energy

Protective shields on ships, planetary protection systems, devices for sending interplanetary packets, ...

Weapons

Weapon systems: torpedoes and missiles, lasers, bombs, ...

Propulsion

Engines, hanging systems for maneuvering in combat, interplanetary gates, ...

Construction

Hulls of ships and orbital stations, armor components, additional fuel and storage tanks, ...

Electronics

Scanners, both planetary and shipborne; missile deflection systems; devices for hiding from enemy scanners; systems for increasing the accuracy and initiative of combat devices; laser amplifiers; ...

Biotechnology

Biotechnologies for the development of terraforming, mines, smart bombs, bio-scanners and bio-armor, ...

In each direction there are 26 levels (or 27, if you count 0 as a level), that is, the technology at any time can be from the 0th to the 26th level. When a race reaches a new level, technological innovations may become available to them: components for ships, types of hulls, planetary defense systems, planetary scanners, expanded capabilities for terraforming planets. In addition, there are positive effects: miniaturization of existing components (what you already know becomes cheaper), increasing the efficiency of the race (AR receives more resources with the development of the Energy direction, JoAT receives better scanners with the development of Electronics).

Each of the directions of technology development can be set in one of three positions:

Costs 75% extra

The development of this science will cost 1.75 times more.

Costs standard amount

Standard cost of science.

Costs 50% less

Science will be 2 times cheaper.

In practice, the meaning of these attitudes determines how quickly a race can reach new levels of science. Accordingly, if science is cheap, then new technological advantages will be available before others.

In addition to all this, a race can have a property set to "All 'Costs 75% extra' research fields start at Tech 3" (or...Tech 4 if you chose JoAT). If this item is missing, the start occurs from standard levels of technology development. If this property is present, then all technologies that were selected above as “Costs 75% extra” start at least from the 3rd (or 4th, if JoAT) level. If the level has already been completed (for example, level 5 Elec when choosing SS), then the item in this branch does not give anything.

This feature works well if the galaxy is small enough and the presence of an initial level of certain technologies is very important.
