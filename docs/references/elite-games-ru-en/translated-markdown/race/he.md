Hyper-Expansion

General description

Hyper-Expansion (HE) - a race focused on rapid expansion in the galaxy. This behavior implies the rapid capture of all possible planets and an accelerated increase in the number of colonists.

Expansion involves the capture of all planets in all corners of the galaxy. This means in most cases the race is incompatible with others.

The race is strong in small galaxies and has a chance of survival in medium-sized galaxies. This is primarily due to the limited speed of hyper-expansion. Long distances become more difficult to overcome over time, and with the development of technology, other types of races are able to provide faster expansion and exploration. Ultimately, HE is unable to concentrate large forces at one point or quickly transfer fleets for attack or defense (due to the lack of gates).

To play HE competently, you need to have experience and a well-thought-out development strategy. HE differs significantly from other types of races both at the stage of race construction and during the game itself, as a result of which development patterns common to all are quite difficult to instill.

Benefits

Double the growth of colonists.

The growth of colonists is 2 times greater than the specified % in the creation of the race. That is, if an increase of 10% is specified, then under ideal conditions the race will give an increase of 20% (for other PRTs it will be 10%).

Unique hull type “Mini Colony Ship”

Unique engine type "Settler's Delight"

Unique "Meta-Morph" body type

Unique component "Flux Capacitor"

Increases the damage of all types of beam weapons by 20% (other races only have the standard Capacitor, which increases by 10%).

Disadvantages

No gates.

The race is unable to build gates of any type.

The maximum population on the planet is 2 times less than that of other races.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 combat scout.

2. 3 Mini Colony Ships.

Starting Science

*All science is basic.

PRT detection

Small colonizers Mini-Colonizer.

These ships appear right at the start of the game and fly to all the planets they can. Their housing is unique to the HE and this clearly defines the HE.

Colonized worlds with a population of less than 2000.

Non-HE colonizers have a capacity of 2500 colonists, and taking into account measurement error and a possible red world, the population of such a planet is at least 2000. There are exceptions as a result of landings on the planet of another race, but these are isolated cases. With the massive appearance of worlds less than 2000, this is HE.

All planets with small populations.

Planets do not have a population greater than 550 thousand (this is the maximum limit for HE).

Population growth is more than 20%.

Only HE can reproduce at a rate of more than 20%. When making this analysis, take into account measurement uncertainty and possible transportation.

Special Flux Capacitor parts and a unique Meta-Morph body.

These elements appear later than the start of the game after studying technologies. Please note that the corps may have been transferred into the possession of another race.

No gates.

Gates are an extremely useful component of orbital stations and it is very surprising that with the appropriate technology, gates do not appear.
