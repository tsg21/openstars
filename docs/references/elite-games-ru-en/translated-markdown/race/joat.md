Jack of All Trades (Generalists)

General description

Jack of All Trades (JOAT) is a versatile race with no major drawbacks. Quite strong and balanced. She is the one who is recommended to train and play for beginners.

The race is very strong economically, and all other things being equal, a very formidable opponent.

Benefits

The population on the planets is larger

On all habitable planets the upper limit is 20% higher.

Built-in scanners

Built-in scanners, both penetrating and conventional.

Choice of Improved Sciences

When choosing improved sciences, sciences move to the 4th level.

Disadvantages

No major drawbacks.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 combat scout.

2. 1 long-distance scout.

3. 1 colonial ship.

4. 1 transport.

5. 1 miner.

6. 1 destroyer.

If the start is at the 4th level of Construction, then the Medium Freighter transport is replaced by a Privateer.

Starting Science

*All levels 3.

PRT detection

Unusual Scout Flight

Scouts fly early on other than planets and even without the scanners themselves.

Complete absence of scanners

Absence on frigates, destroyers and scouts.

There are more than 1,100 thousand people on the planets

Since it has a 20% bonus on population.

Notes

Built-in scanners are built into all Scout-class ships.

The built-in scanner has ranges that depend on the current level in Elec. Calculation formula for penetrating built-in scanners:

R = (10 * {Elec Level})ly

Conventional scanners are also built-in. Their radius is 2 times larger, and accordingly 4 times larger when the NAS is turned on.
