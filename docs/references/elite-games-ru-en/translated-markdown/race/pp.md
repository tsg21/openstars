Packet Physics

General description

Packet Physics (PP) - a race that specializes in the construction and use of packet engines that allow you to move cargo without the participation of transports and bombard enemy planets with packages.

Benefits

2 starting planets

If the galaxy is larger than “Tiny”, then the race starts with 2 planets, one of which is HW.

Unique orbital engine designs: Driver 5,6,8,9,11,12,13

Engines are installed only on the station and they are much cheaper than those of other races.

Packages have a built-in scanner

The packages scan planets and ships with a built-in penetrating scanner.

Building packages is cheaper

Packs are cheaper to build and are built in smaller batches.

Packets visible in the galaxy

PP sees at any moment all flying packages in the galaxy, regardless of whether they are visible by scanners or not.

Study of orbital station design in a package

If an enemy orbital station receives a PP packet, then the design of the orbital station becomes known.

Terraforming in batches

When a PP package hits a planet, it has a chance to be terraformed.

Packages degrade more slowly in flight

When running packages in PP are destroyed, they are destroyed 2 times slower.

Disadvantages

Dear race by points

Has a fairly large negative balance of points and is therefore economically weaker.

Starting Fleet

1. 2 scouts.

2. 1 colonial ship.

3. 1 transport.

Starting Science

* Level 4 in Energy.

PRT detection

Unique engines.

Availability of exotic engines on bases. Usually PP build unique engines because they are cheaper for them.

Early game packages.

PP uses packets early on to scan planets.

Notes

Packages are serious weapons. Its use forces all neighbors to protect their planets to the fullest.

At launch, both planets have a basic package engine. On one planet there is a starbase, on another there is a fort.

For built-in scanners in packages, the scanning radius is the distance that the package flies in 1 year.
