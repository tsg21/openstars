Claim Adjuster (Terraformists)

General description

Claim Adjuster (CA) is a race of terraformers. They instantly transform the living conditions of the planet in their own way, and for other races they can make living conditions unbearable.

Benefits

Instant Terraform

When a planet is populated or a new level in terraform is opened, the planet is automatically terraformed to suit the natural living conditions of the race.

Probable terraform

Every year a planet inhabited by CA has a probability (1 in 10 per year) of a change in the native natural conditions of the planet (one of the random ones) in favor of the race.

Unique weapon: Retro-bomb

The bomb "deterraforms" the planet to its native values.

Unique component: Orbital Adjuster

Miner-class devices modify the planet's environment from orbit.

Disadvantages

No major drawbacks.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 combat scout.

2. 1 colonial ship.

3. 1 ship for terraforming the planets of other players.

Starting Science

* Level 6 in Biotech.

* Level 1 in Weapons.

* Level 1 in Propulsion.

* Level 1 in Energy.

PRT detection

Instant Terraform

Very visible at any stage of the game. It is almost impossible to make a quick terraform for an ordinary race.

CA Components

Bombers and terraformers are very noticeable, both in design and in tonnage.

Notes

If the CA leaves the planet or the planet is captured, then the living conditions automatically return to their native ones.

The terraforming ship transforms friendly planets for the better (for itself or for a friendly race), and neutral and enemy planets, on the contrary, worsen them.
