Secondary race traits - Lesser Race Traits (LRT)

Secondary race skills that are selective, meaning each of them can either be selected or not selected. They affect the gameplay less than PRT, but quite significantly.

Description

There are 14 different LRTs, the choice of which is made in the 3rd step of creating a race. The LRTs are as follows:

Improved Fuel Efficiency (IFE) - improved fuel use.

1. Gives the Fuel Mizer engine.

2. Reduces fuel consumption by 15%.

3. If NRSE is not selected, then the Galaxy Scoop engine is given.

4. Gives +1 Propulsion level at the start of the game.

Total Terraforming (TT) - total, complete terraforming.

1. Makes it possible to improve the ability to terraform planets with the study of biotechnology. These are additional terraformings ±3, ±5, ±10, ±15, ±20, ±25, ±30 with the study of levels 0, 6, 9, 13, 17, 22, 25, respectively.

2. Reducing the cost of terraform by 30% (70 instead of 100 resources by 1%).

3. When TT is turned on, the science menu does not show terraforms that are not TT. But these terraforms are triggered and executed in the game.

Advanced Remote Mining (ARM) - improved remote mining.

1. The race is given 3 additional digger designs (Midget Miner, Miner, Ultra-Miner).

2. The race is given 2 additional types of digging robots (Robo-Midget Miner, Robo-Ultra-Miner).

3. At the start, a pair (2) of additional mining ships are given.

Don't choose an ARM over an OBRM.

Improved Starbases (ISB) - improved orbital stations.

1. 2 additional starbase designs are given: Space Dock and Ultra Station.

2. The cost of orbital stations and their components is 20% cheaper.

3. A 20% cloak is automatically built into all orbital stations.

Highly recommended for the AR race, but the 20% cost upgrade does not apply to them.

Generalized Research (GR) - decentralized study of sciences.

When studying Sciences, the race invests 50% in the chosen category, and the remaining technologies receive 15% each. When reaching the maximum technological development in a branch (level 26), these resources in the branch will be lost.

For 15% and 50% parts it is always rounded up. Those. for example, if you spend 1 resource on science, you will get +1 for all sciences.

It is considered a negative property, since most often it is necessary to concentrate in one direction instead of being scattered across all sciences.

Ultimated Recycling (UR) - Improved recycling.

Improves the disassembly of ships in orbit. With this skill, more minerals remain after disassembly and, in addition, some resources are freed up.

In practice, large-scale use of UR by resources is impossible. Since Stars! When scraping more than 1 ship over a planet, the allocated resources are reduced, and a reduced number of resources is observed if the planet is weak in production capacity.

Mineral Alchemy (MA) - alchemy of minerals.

Allows you to obtain minerals from resources 4 times more efficiently (25 resources per 1 kt of each mineral instead of 100).

No Ram Scoop Engines (NRSE) - no Ramscoop engines.

1. Prohibits all Ramscoop class engines (capable of receiving fuel while flying in space) except Fuel Mizer (if IFE is selected) and Enigma Pulsar (can only be obtained from MT).

2. Gives the Interspace-10 engine, allowing much earlier Ramscoop engines to fly safely at 10th speed.

Cheap Engines (CE) - cheap engines.

1. Engines are 2 times cheaper.

2. When flying above speed 6, the engines have a 10% chance of not starting.

3. Gives 1 additional Proplusion level at the start.

Only Basic Remote Mining (OBRM) - only basic remote mining.

1. Mining ships are limited to the Mini-Miner hull.

2. Digging robots are limited to the Robo-Mini-Miner component. It is not prohibited to receive Alien Miner from MT.

3. The maximum population on planets is increased by 10% (the property is additive for JOAT and HE).

No Advanced Scanners (NAS) - no advanced scanners.

1. All penetrating scanners, both planetary stationary and those installed on ships, are missing. The property does not prohibit Chameleon Scanner, Robber Baron Scanner, scanners built into JOAT and scanners from MT.

2. Conventional scanners have double the scanning radius.

Low Starting Population (LSP) - reduced population at the start.

30% smaller population at the start of the game.

Bleeding Edge Technology (BET) - bleeding advanced technologies.

All advanced technologies (if the element is completely at the advanced technology levels according to the requirements) cost 2 times more. But at the same time, miniaturization improves to 80% and 5% per level (instead of 75% and 4% per level).

Regenerating Shields (RS) - regenerating shields.

1. All shields are 40% stronger.

2. During the battle, each turn, shields are restored by 10%.

3. Additional armor on ships is 2 times weaker.

LRT detection

IFE

1. Availability of FM engine.

2. Calculation of fuel consumption (especially for the case if the ship was stuck without fuel and its trajectory was visible).

3. Availability of GS engine.

TT

1. The terraforms are off scale.

2. A clear shift of terraform by +5, +7, +10 in the absence of corresponding science.

3. Faster terraform.

ARM

1. At the start, the basic digging machines are visible.

2. Availability of advanced mining robots or housings.

ISB

1. Availability of “Space Dock” or “Ultra Station”.

2. 20% cloak for any orbital station.

GR

1. Using SS spying.

UR

According to economic point indicators of use. The LRT itself is very rarely used.

M.A.

According to economic point indicators of use. The LRT itself is very rarely used.

NRSE

1. Availability of Interspace-10 engine.

2. Complete absence of Ramscoop.

C.E.

1. Frequent false starts of engines.

2. Point indicators of ship dismantling.

OBRM

1. Population increased by 10%.

2. Complete absence of remote digging.

NAS

1. Distant detection (especially clearly visible for cloaked ships).

2. There are no penetrating scanners on board.

LSP

Small population at the start. It is detected only at the very start of the game.

BET

Rare use of advanced technologies when available. The LRT itself is very rarely used.

R.S.

In combat, strong shields and/or weak additional armor.
