Super Stealth

General description

Super Stealth (SS) - the race allows for a secretive lifestyle - its ships are more difficult to detect in space using scanners.

SS has a large number of unique things that are different in nature and significance. Components appear at different stages of the game and require the development of different types of technology, which makes playing as this race more interesting.

The SS race is economically weaker. This means that in a dual game in the scenario “whoever builds a larger fleet can defeat it in a big battle wins,” the SS simply loses. This race needs to maneuver competently, make targeted strikes, raids behind enemy lines, unexpectedly capture key planets, etc. With good preparation and thoughtful play, even experienced Stars players! This race can cause a lot of unexpected headaches.

Benefits

Built-in 75% cloak

Any ship belonging to the SS race (even those transferred from non-SS) has an internal camouflage system built into it at 75% cloak. Also, this system works for orbital stations, but does not work for mineral packages, salvage, planets.

Technology espionage

Each year, SS races receive a science bonus equal to half the average amount of resources spent from everyone (including themselves).

Safer movement through minefields

The race has 1 higher “safe speed” of movement through minefields.

Unique body: Rogue

A multifunctional body designed by its creators for conducting pirate operations in deep space.

Unique body: Stealth Bomber

An improved bomber with special slots for electronic devices and an expanded fuel tank.

Unique Shield: Shadow Shield

Shield with built-in cloak.

Unique armor: Depleted Neutronim

Armor with built-in cloak.

Unique scanner: Chameleon Scanner

Scanner with built-in cloak.

Unique scanner: Pick Pocket Scanner

A scanner that allows you to rob cargo from ships. To rob, you must be at the same point as the ship.

Unique scanner: Robber Baron Scanner

This scanner allows you to rob minerals directly from the surface of planets (including alien ones, regardless of the presence of an orbital station in orbit).

Unique concealment device: Transport Cloaking

A cheap cloak device, based on basic technologies, can only be built into Transport class ships. To rob, you must be in orbit of the planet.

Unique concealment device: Ultra-Stealth Cloak

The most powerful concealment device.

Notes

The additional cargo of transports does not affect camouflage.

Espionage - 50% of the total average per player. That is, if there are N players, and someone spends R resources, then espionage will give R/(2*N) resources, rounded down.

Espionage counts for all active races. If a race has left the game, then the average number of resources is calculated without its participation. That is, if there are N living races in the game, then SS spy 1/(2*N) part of the resources.

Espionage only counts for resources invested by races into science. Technology transfers and MT are not included in this calculation.

If someone spends R resources, but some of them go to one research field, and some to another (for example, when setting “next field”), then all espionage resources are counted as in the starting field, that is, R/(2*N) will be received in the starting field.

If a race uses GR, then espionage is considered as a cost for all technology branches at the beginning of the year. If the “next field” changes, then spying occurs only for the starting field.

If there is only one SS race in the galaxy, then the espionage bonus is not awarded to it.
