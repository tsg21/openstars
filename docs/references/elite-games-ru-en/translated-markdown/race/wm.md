War Monger

General description

War Monger (WM) is a combat attacking race. She is a master of war and she has to do this all the time. It has a number of features for a stronger attack, but there are a number of disadvantages that significantly weaken the defense.

WM is a good race for small games and can also be played mediocre in medium to long games. A good and fast start is ensured by starting science, increased mobility in battle and a good landing attack. At the same time, weakened protection does not appear at the start. In the middle stage, additional designs work well, and in a protracted game, cheap weapons already show themselves, allowing you to build a larger fleet.

In the game, the race is characterized by the fact that it needs to attack often, and start doing this very early. It is difficult to defend with such a race, so WM should always hold the initiative.

Benefits

Ships move faster in battle.

They move 0.5 squares per round faster than other races. This allows you to quickly overtake the enemy or leave the battle.

Automatic ship design detection

They immediately see the design of the ship if it was noticed in any way (scanner, the presence of its own planet in orbit).

Cheap weapons

All Weapons class components cost 25% less.

Enhanced airborne attack

If WM lands on a regular race, then the attack is 50% stronger.

Unique Hull: Battle Cruiser

A more powerful modification of the Cruiser.

Unique Hull: Dreadnought

A more powerful modification of Battleship.

Unique Weapon: Gatling Neutrino Cannon

A weapon for clearing mines and destroying large numbers of groups of ships.

Unique Weapon: Blunderbuss

The most powerful beam weapon in the game.

Disadvantages

No min

The race is unable to build mines of any type.

Weak planetary defense

Planetary defense can be either SDI or at most Missile.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 combat scout.

2. 1 colonial ship.

If you start with level 3 in Construction, then a destroyer and a mini-bomber are added to the initial fleet.

Starting Science

* Level 6 in Weapons.

* Level 1 in Propulsion.

* Level 1 in Energy.

PRT detection

Movement speed in battle

The speed of all WM ships is 0.5 higher. Usually one battle is enough to determine that the enemy ship moves more than its allotted number of cells.

WM components and housings.

Notes

Faster movement in combat is limited to a limit of 2.5 cells, as for other races.
