Alternate Reality

General description

Alternate Reality (AR) - representatives of this race do not live on the surface of planets, but in orbit in orbital stations.

This gives rise to many features: they do not have factories or mines, a dynamic scanner, etc..

The race can only be played on small maps or in an alliance under protection. This is due to the fact that the orbital station in the game is very easy to shoot down and, as a result, lose the planet.

It can be used often in allied games, as it has endless reserves of minerals.

Benefits

Population determines the level of mines and factories

Mines and factories are "built" automatically as the population increases.

Built-in scanner

The planetary scanner is built into the orbital station.

The ability to dig your own planets

It is possible to dig for planets directly from an inhabited orbit.

Infinite minerals

It is possible to build diggers at an infinite number of HWs, and as a result, an infinite number of mined minerals.

Unique colonizer

Can bomb enemy planets and automatically turns into a fort in orbit.

Cheap orbital stations

Orbital stations are 20% cheaper.

The maximum population is determined by the type of orbital station, not the planet

This allows for a population of well over 1 million.

Planetary resources increase as Energy level increases.

Packet protected

The received package does not cause damage resulting in the loss of colonists.

Unique Starbase: Death Star

The largest orbital station in the game.

Disadvantages

Cannot build planetary buildings

Everything happens in orbit.

Colonists die in outer space

When in open space, colonists die 3% per turn.

Starting Fleet

1. 1 scout.

2. 1 colonial ship.

Starting Science

* Level 1 in Energy.

PRT detection

SS espionage

Early investment in energy - right from the start.

The colonizer is 18 kT heavier

The colonizer has a module that is heavier than the usual one.

The colonizer is bombing your planet

Colonizer AR bombs enemy planets, removing 2,000 colonists per year.

Transports are made easier in flight

When moving, AR colonists die.

Planet with ??? colonists

The scanner cannot determine the number of colonists, so it displays ???.

Notes

Starbases are 20% cheaper and this cannot be combined with ISB.

Diggers also dig during gating.

Planetary scanners vary depending on the type of housing.

The range of such scanners depends on the number of people in the orbital station and the type of station.

Penetrating scanners are present in Ultra Station and Death Star; in other types of cases only regular scanners are present.
