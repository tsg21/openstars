Primary Race Traits (PRT)

Each race has its own PRT. It determines the main features of the race, its advantages and weaknesses in the game. Most often, the entire game strategy for both individual races and alliances is built on the basis of the choice of PRT.

The choice of PRT is made at the 2nd step of creating a race. There are 10 PRTs:

[Hyper-Expansion (HE)]
Author: Minx

Rapid expansion in the galaxy.

[Super Stealth (SS)]
Author: Minx

Improved invisibility abilities.

[War Monger (WM)]
Author: Minx

An attacking race, strong in attack and weapons, weak in defense.

[Claim Adjuster (CA)]
Author: Minx

Focus on terraforming - changing the conditions of the planets to suit your needs.

[Inner-Strength (IS)]
Author: Minx

Defensive race, difficult to attack and easier to defend.

[Space Demolition (SD)]
Author: Minx

Masters of laying minefields.

[Packet Physics (PP)]
Author: Minx

Specialization in package engines.

[Interstellar Traveler (IT)]
Author: Minx

Special abilities and capabilities in gating of ships and cargo.

[Alternate Reality (AR)]
Author: Minx

The race does not live on the surface of planets, but in orbit in starbases.

[Jack of All Traders (JoAT)]
Author: Minx

Without serious disadvantages and strong advantages.
