Recommendations

If you:

Silicanoid

Build as much Spore Cloud as you can and be the first in the early years of the game, as later on it will be difficult for you to play effectively.

Nucleotid

Find and terraform the greenest planets (up to 100%)

Since you have expensive technology, take advantage of the rapid population growth.

Insectoid

Take advantage of a good level of weapons as quickly as possible, as you will feel a lack of resources in the future.

Antetheral

Place minefields or lose.

Explode fields and take minerals - otherwise you will lose.

Rabbitoid

Activate the study of Construction technology - you need Any/300 gates, and after that you will begin to move fleets as you please in the galaxy.

Humanoid

You can do everything equally well.

Harass and pester your enemies to death.
