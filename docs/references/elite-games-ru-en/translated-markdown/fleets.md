Fleets

Fleets (squadrons) are the main mobile units in the game.

They differ from ships in that any number of ships can be combined into one squadron. A single ship is also a fleet. Merging is possible during the course of a move or using the “Merge with Fleet” order.

If ships are grouped into one squadron, then they can only be given 1 type of order at point wp0 and another order at point wp1. Therefore, keeping miners laying mines, transports doing loading and unloading, miners extracting minerals in the fleet... will not be possible. Also, only one battleplan can be given to the fleet. Therefore, to separate orders and functions, it is recommended to split fleets accordingly.

Ships united in a squadron are easier to manage and have their own advantages. They consume fuel together, take a hit in a minefield at once, are visible to the enemy as one structural unit (therefore cannot be destroyed when leaving in bulk or breaking up into small ships), are accepted by MT completely by the entire squadron, and in battle, ships of the same type in the fleet act as one united group.

Based on the features of Stars!, there can be no more than 256 fleets at any point in space and this should be kept in mind.

If at one point in the galaxy there are 2 hostile fleets or a hostile fleet + orbital station, and one of the pair is able to attack the second and has an attacking battleplan, then a battle occurs. All squadrons that are present at a given point (even random ships that were not in action) participate in the battle.

One fleet can have no more than 32,000 ships. However, a player cannot have more than 512 fleets.
