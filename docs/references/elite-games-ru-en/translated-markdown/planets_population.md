Planetary population

Each colony is characterized by its population. These are representatives of the race who can perform various economic functions.

First of all, colonists bring resources. More colonists means the colony produces more resources, and with the help of resources everything else is done - buildings, ships are built, technologies are studied, etc.

The second important function is that colonists control planetary buildings. These could be factories, mines, protective structures. More colonists means more buildings can be controlled, and accordingly they perform their functions more efficiently.

The next important property of colonists is that under favorable conditions they reproduce, but under bad climate they die. In the first case, this means generating resources and increasing the overall control of buildings, in the second - the slow death of the colony.

And the last property of colonists is that they are transportable. You can at any time pick up colonists from one planet into the hold of a transport (100 colonists occupy 1 kt) and take them to another planet. This allows the race to spread throughout the galaxy.

Colonization of planets

In order to populate an uninhabited planet, it must be colonized. If the planet is not inhabited, then there is no other way to use it in your economy (except for remote digging).

For colonization, a squadron is required, which must include 1 ship with a colonization module. In addition, this squadron must carry at least 1 kt of colonists. If the described conditions are met, this squadron can carry out colonization. In the simplest case, this is a single ship with a built-in colonization module and a cargo compartment with colonists.

To colonize, you need to send a ship to the planet and set the order “Colonize”. You can give an order for colonization while in orbit of a planet, in which case a colony will also be created.

As a result of colonization, the entire fleet that was ordered to "Colonize" is dismantled. 75% of the minerals necessary for the construction of the colonization fleet, as well as 100% of the cargo that was brought in the hold (colonists + minerals), reach the surface of the planet.

If colonization of two races occurs on the planet at the same time, then a battle for the planet occurs between them. The race with more colonists occupies the planet, losing the number of colonists of the other race. If a planet is colonized by an equal number of colonists, then no one will get it. It will remain uninhabited, and the minerals will remain on the surface.

If a planet is colonized for the first time, a random event may occur: the discovery of an artifact.

Ground battles

If the planet does not have an orbital station, then an assault force can be landed on it by another race. This action is performed by the “Unload” order for transport or using direct unloading with the “Xfer” button.

As a result of this action, a ground battle occurs. In the simplest case, this is a simple calculation of which race is larger and, accordingly, the race that has more representatives survives. For example, if there are 10,000 people on a planet, and 13,100 colonists land on it, then it will be captured and the invaders will have 3,100 colonists left. If the defending race is stronger, then it will suffer corresponding losses.

IS races are protected 2 times more effectively. WM races attack 1.5 times more efficiently.

Planetary defense against landings can be improved with planetary defense.

Diagnosed number of colonists living on the planet

When viewing planets other than your own Stars! show what their population is. This number is given each year with a 20% error.
