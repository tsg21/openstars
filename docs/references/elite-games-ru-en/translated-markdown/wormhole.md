Spatial holes (Wormhole)

Spatial holes are independent objects in the game.

These are paired objects, that is, one hole is connected to another hole at any given time. It has the property that if you fly into one hole, the fleet will appear instantly from the other (paired) hole.

Shown here are 3 holes (black and purple circular objects). Two pairs and an entrance to one hole, the exit from which is unknown.

If you fly into one hole and fly out of another, scanning the exit, the pair becomes known and is connected by a straight line - this indicates that the player knows the connection between the holes. If the connection is unknown, then the hole is shown as a single object.

The transition between holes is bidirectional - if you fly in from one and appear on the other side, then the same flight is possible in the other direction. At the same time, ships of any mass can be thrown through the hole.

The location and the destination when jumping are shown, which can be unknown. Stability shows the dynamic state of the hole, which affects the time it takes for the hole to close. This state ranges from "Rock Solid" to "Extremely Volatile". "Rock Solid" is the state of the hole when it is created and changes through the states "Rock Solid" → "Stable" → "Mostly Stable" → "Average" → "Slightly Volatile" → "Volatile" → "Extremely Volatile". The hole can close or switch after the “Average” state at any time. And it can remain in the “Extremely Volatile” state for quite a long time (tens of years).

The hole can appear or is only in space.

Behavior of black holes

Every year the entrance/exit of the hole moves in space by a certain number ly. Over time, the hole may close, appear in another point in the galaxy, or switch to another hole.

The hole always changes its position once a year. In this case, each of the inputs and outputs changes its position independently.

The number of holes depends on the size of the galaxy—the larger, the more holes may be present. Holes live from the beginning of the game and their number does not change during the game.

Scanning black holes

Each of the inputs and outputs is unknown at the beginning of the game and is under 75% cloak for standard scanners. Penetrating scanners see without cloak.

If a hole has been scanned and found once, then it loses 75% cloak.

If the hole closes, then the new point becomes 75% cloak.
