Functional purpose of ships

According to their functional purpose, ships can be divided into the following types:

Scout

The main task is to obtain information about a potential enemy, doing it covertly if possible and without losing the reconnaissance ship itself. Of significant importance are:

1. Scanning radius of both conventional and penetrating lasers.

2. Fuel capacity and the ability to fly far from bases.

3. Speed ​​to avoid the enemy both in space and in battle.

4. Cloak, which makes it possible to conduct reconnaissance unnoticed by the enemy.

Colonizer

A ship for the colonization of planets. Must have a module for colonization, and preferably the ability to transport at least a handful of colonists.

Transport

The main purpose is to transport goods and colonists. For such ships you need:

1. Maximum tonnage of transported cargo.

2. Sufficient fuel supply.

3. High speed of transportation.

Warship

These ships are exclusively engaged in the destruction of enemy ships and orbital stations. For them, the main thing is to survive in battle and the ability to destroy escort ships. For such purposes the following are essential:

1. Combat power of weapons.

2. Weapon range.

3. Speed ​​in battle.

4. Weapons Initiative.

5. Armor and shields.

6. Compliance with tonnage restrictions for movement through gates.

Repair and fuel transportation ship (engineering, SFX)

SFX - Super-Fuel Xport. Designed to accompany the fleet - performing repair work after battles, and also transport significant reserves of fuel and generate the fuel itself. For SFX it is important:

1. Be in the fleet in at least 1 unit.

2. Be able to move with the main fleet at an appropriate speed.

Bomber (bomber)

This type of ship is engaged in bombing planets with enemy populations. Based on this, he needs, first of all, the ability to destroy: the population (to capture) and planetary buildings (to suppress planetary defenses).

Minelayer (miner)

Such ships lay minefields. For them, the main thing is the ability to quickly place many mines in a given area. For such purposes the following are essential:

1. Installation speed min.

2. Stealth, if this is done in the enemy area.

Mineral miner (digger)

The ship is designed to mine minerals on distant planets. For this variety the following matters:

1. Digging ability (number of mines in the equivalent of a mineshaft).

2. Ability to get through gates.

3. The ability to be hidden from long-range penetrating enemy scanners.

Stealth Concealer

The purpose is to hide other ships from enemy scanners during sudden and covert operations. Such a ship is in the same fleet with others and hides them. It requires:

1. High level of cloak.

2. Large mass (by any means).

Technology transmitter (giver)

Built to be quickly dismantled or destroyed for the benefit of whoever adopts the technology. It should be easy and cheap to build, quickly and without significant losses delivered to the desired point, and contain a component for technology transfer.

Here are the types common to all races. Exotic cases were not considered.

In general, a ship can be designed to perform multiple functions. But it is recommended that the ship perform one function and do it well.
