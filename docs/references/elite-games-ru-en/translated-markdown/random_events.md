Random Events

Random events include:

1. The fall of comets on planets.

2. Discovery of new minerals.

3. Fundamental changes in planetary climate.

4. The emergence of MT.

5. The appearance and presence of holes.

6. Finding artifacts on planets.

If the “No Random Events” flag is set when creating a game, then none of the above events will occur.

Each of the 1, 2, 3, 4 events in the list can occur only 1 time in 1 year. Events 1, 2, 3 occur before the production and after the extraction of minerals.

Fall of comets

The impact of a comet on a planet brings a large amount of minerals (which will be on the surface) and leads to an increase in the concentration of minerals on the planet. That's where the good news from the comet crash ends. The comet destroys part of the population (if there is one, but defenses do not save) and cancels the production queue (all unfinished work is lost, including resources spent on that work).

Comets come in different sizes. Depending on the size, the magnitude of the consequences varies. Comets fall with a probability of 5% per year throughout the entire galaxy, while each size of the comet is equally likely and the fall occurs with equal probability for each of the planets. The fall of a comet becomes known to everyone in the galaxy.

Comets bring various minerals, and small comets can bring typical types of minerals.

Small comets have one type of mineral, that is, such comets are ironium, boranium and germanium. This means that they carry mostly this typical mineral.

Medium comets always have two typical minerals, meaning they can carry I+B, I+G or B+G.

Comets Large and Huge carry minerals of all types.

Discovery of new minerals

The discovery of new minerals occurs only on inhabited planets. The discovery implies an increase in the concentration of minerals on the planet without changing anything else. An event occurs in the galaxy with a probability of 2.5% per year and becomes known to those to whom it happens. The event is triggered only for one type of mineral and increases its concentration by a random number of points from 5 to 20.

Fundamental changes in planetary climate

A fundamental change in the planet's climate involves a change in one or more climatic parameters by several points, while the original value changes. The event occurs in the galaxy with a probability of 2.5% per year, occurs only on an inhabited planet and becomes known to the one to whom it occurs. Such an event changes one of the parameters of the native climate value by a value from 5 to 8 points in a positive or negative direction. It cannot go beyond the edge of the value; in this case it stops at the extreme (less than 1 mR, more than 7.76g, ...).

Emergence of MT

The appearance of MT occurs with a probability of 8% and can occur ~ from the 3rd-4th decade of the game. MT appears flying out from the edge of the galaxy and its movement can be anything, but most often it is linear.

The appearance and presence of holes

Holes appear already at the start of the game in random places. During the game they can appear and disappear.

Finding artifacts on planets

If a planet is colonized for the first time, then with a 1/3 probability an artifact may be discovered on it. An artifact implies the receipt of a certain number of resources in one of the branches of technology.

For artifacts, the calculation of the resources received is as follows:

{Number of resources} = Colonists / 1000 * {Random number from 100 to 400}

, where Colonists is the number of colonists in the colonizing fleet, but this number is considered to be no more than 1000.

You can get from 100 to 400 resources if the colonization fleet contains 1000 or more colonists.

If STA is selected when creating a game, then the artifact will actually receive 2 times more resources: see the STA bug.
