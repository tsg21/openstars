Home world (HW)

All players start the game in their home worlds. These planets are called HW and have their own characteristics.

At the start, each player's HW, whether it belongs to an AI or a human, is equipped similarly depending on the starting properties of the races. HW has at least 10 mines, factories, defenses and a planetary scanner.

At the start of the game, all planets contain minerals and all HWs start with the same mineral content on the surface, adjusted to the advantages or disadvantages of each race. If you have a serious deficiency of a specific mineral on the surface, then your opponents have the same problems.

HW have an unlimited number of minerals and their concentration does not fall below 30 during mining.

To detect HW, you need to scan the planet and click the left mouse button on the mineral content form. If “(30)” is indicated in the concentration, then this planet was someone’s HW.
